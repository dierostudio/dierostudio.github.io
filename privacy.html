1. Who we are

Vice Life Cortes ("the tool") is a personal desktop application used only by the owner of the Vice Life channels to publish short video clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

2. Information the tool accesses

When the owner authorizes the tool with TikTok or YouTube, the tool receives:

  • An access token that allows it to act on the owner's own account.
  • Basic account information, such as the display name, used only to confirm which account is connected.

The tool does not request, collect or store information about any other user.

3. How the information is used

The access token is used only to upload and publish video clips to the owner's own account. The display name is used only to show the owner which account is connected.

4. Where the information is stored

Tokens are stored only in local files on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than the official TikTok and Google (YouTube) services.

5. Sharing

The tool does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings, and can delete the local token files. Once revoked, the tool can no longer access the account.

7. Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from anyone.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
