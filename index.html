About

Vice Life Cortes is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of the Vice Life channels to turn moments from authorized GTA and GTA RP live streams into short vertical videos.

The tool records the live stream on the owner's own computer, cuts the best moments, adds the Vice Life frame and subtitles, and publishes the finished clip to the owner's own accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit: the owner logs in with their own TikTok account to authorize the tool.
  • Content Posting API: the tool sends the finished clip to the owner's TikTok account, either as a draft for review or as a post on the owner's own profile.

The tool is used only by the account owner. It does not access, collect or post anything for other users.
